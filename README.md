# Brand Build Digital

Build a premium, modern, high-converting website for my IT & Digital Marketing agency called “[AGENCY NAME]”.

IMPORTANT:

This website is NOT a generic template. It should look like a professional international agency that works with clients from the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and other countries.

The main goal of this website is to:

1. Showcase our services

2. Build trust with international clients

3. Showcase our portfolio/case studies

4. Generate leads

5. Make visitors book a call or contact us

6. Clearly communicate that we build websites and help businesses grow their online presence

TARGET CLIENTS:

Small and medium-sized businesses, startups, local businesses and business owners in the UK, USA, Canada and Australia.

SERVICES WE OFFER:

- Website Design & Development

- Landing Page Design

- E-commerce Website Development

- Website Redesign

- SEO

- Google Ads / PPC

- Social Media Management

- Instagram Growth & Content Strategy

- Social Media Marketing

- Google Business Profile Optimization

- Website Maintenance & Support

DESIGN DIRECTION:

Create a premium SaaS/modern digital agency aesthetic.

The website should feel:

- Premium

- Clean

- Modern

- Trustworthy

- International

- Professional

- Conversion-focused

- Minimal but visually impressive

Avoid:

- Cheap-looking agency designs

- Excessive gradients

- Too many animations

- Generic stock-template appearance

- Huge paragraphs

- Overcrowded sections

Use strong typography, excellent spacing, modern cards, subtle borders, clean icons, tasteful animations and professional visual hierarchy.

COLOR STYLE:

Use a sophisticated modern color palette with a light background and dark typography, with one strong accent color for CTAs and important elements.

Make sure the design has excellent contrast and accessibility.
